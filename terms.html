Hopewick Terms of Use

Last updated: 19 October 2026

These are the rules for using Hopewick. Please read them. By using Hopewick, you agree to them.

Hopewick is run by BRIDGE&BITE.CO PTY LTD (ABN 83 692 080 792). "We" and "us" means that company.

What Hopewick is (and is not)

  • Hopewick is an app to support people in recovery.
  • It has daily readings, check-ins, a journal, goals, a safety plan, relapse and exit plans, a recovery tracker, learning modules, Move & Recover, and a chat helper called Hope.
  • Faith content is optional. It stays off unless you switch it on, and you can switch it off any time.
  • Hope is a computer program (AI). It is not a person.
  • Hope can get things wrong. Check anything important.
  • Hopewick is not a doctor, counsellor or medical service.
  • Hopewick does not give medical advice.
  • Move & Recover is general information about movement, sauna and cold water, not medical advice. Its safety check is not a medical assessment. If you are unsure, check with your GP, physio or exercise physiologist.
  • Hopewick is not a crisis service. Nobody watches chats. We will not call anyone for you.
  • In danger or crisis? Call 000. Or call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

Who can use it

  • You must be 13 or older.
  • If you are 13, 14 or 15, a parent or guardian must say it's okay. By signing up, you tell us they have.
  • Parents and guardians: you are responsible for your child's use of Hopewick, including any payments.
  • You must give us a real email address.
  • Keep your password safe. Don't share your account.

Cost and payment

  • Free plan: costs nothing. Up to 15 Hope messages a day. You see your latest chat, and the last 7 days of your history in the app. Nothing older is deleted, and you can always export it.
  • Plus: AU$20 a month. No daily message limit (fair use applies). You can open older chats and use extra tools.
  • Free trial: your first Plus plan has a 7-day free trial. You won't be charged if you cancel before it ends.
  • Payment is taken by card through Stripe each month until you cancel.
  • Our Android app is still in review with Google Play, and we don't take payments there yet. Once it launches on Google Play, Google Play billing will apply to subscriptions bought in that app, and we'll update these terms first.
  • Cancel anytime in the app. Plus keeps working until the end of the month you paid for.
  • If you delete your account, your subscription stops straight away.
  • If Plus ends, you keep everything you saved. You can still see it, export it and delete it.
  • Refunds: we don't usually refund part-months. But you always keep your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If something is badly wrong, you may get a refund.
  • We will tell you before we change the price.

What we do

  • We try to keep Hopewick working and safe.
  • We protect your information as our Privacy Policy says. Much of what you save is health information, which the law treats as sensitive. Our Your data page explains where it's kept and how to export or delete it.
  • We will answer your emails.
  • Sometimes Hopewick may be down for repairs or problems.

What you agree to do

  • Use Hopewick for yourself, in a lawful way.
  • Not try to hack, break or overload the app.
  • Not use Hope to hurt or harass anyone.
  • Not copy or resell our content.
  • Seek real help from a doctor or a crisis line when you need it.

Ending or changing the agreement

  • You can stop at any time. Delete your account in My Account.
  • We may close your account if you break these rules. We will tell you why, unless it is unsafe to do so.
  • We may change these terms. If it is a big change, we will tell you first. If you don't agree, you can stop using Hopewick.
  • We may change or stop Hopewick. If we stop it, we will give you notice and stop charging you.

If something goes wrong

  1. Email us at hello@hopewick.com.au.
  2. We aim to acknowledge it within 2 business days and resolve it within 10 business days. Those are targets, not a guarantee.
  3. Still not fixed? You can contact your state's fair trading office (in Queensland: the Office of Fair Trading).
  4. For privacy problems, you can complain in writing to the OAIC (oaic.gov.au, enquiries 1300 363 992) if you are not happy with our answer.
  5. These terms follow the law of Queensland, Australia.

Our responsibility (limits)

  • Under the Australian Consumer Law, you have rights that we cannot take away. Nothing in these terms takes away those rights.
  • Apart from those rights, Hopewick is given "as it is".
  • We are not responsible for choices you make based on what Hope says.
  • If the law lets us, our total responsibility to you is limited to giving the service again, or paying back what you paid us in the last 12 months.
