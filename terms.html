Terms

These terms describe the service as it works today. They are written in plain language for people in Australia.

The service

Hopewick is operated by BRIDGE&BITE.CO PTY LTD. Hope is an AI recovery companion for the hours between appointments. Hope is not a doctor, psychologist, registered counsellor, clinician, or emergency service. Chats are not monitored. Hopewick does not replace professional treatment or emergency care.

If you need urgent help, call 000. Other Australian contacts are on the safety section of the homepage.

Who it is for

People in recovery, people thinking about change, families, and Australian AOD services and rehabs. Faith content is optional. Word for the day is separate from Just for today. Hopewick is not only for Christians.

Free and Plus

The free plan does not need a card. It includes 15 Hope messages a day, crisis and practical support, today’s readings, the daily check-in, the clean-time tracker, and the latest saved chat when you are signed in.

Hopewick Plus is AU$20 a month. It has no daily message cap. It includes opening older stored chats, SMART goals, the journal, relapse and exit plans, the resume builder, the recovery learning library, Going Deeper study tracks, and the full year of readings. One subscription per person.

Seat plans for rehabs, AOD services and clinics are not on sale yet.

The trial, the card, and later checkouts

The first subscription only is a 7-day trial. A card is required. Stripe collects it at the start. You are not charged the monthly price during the trial. The AU$20 charge starts when the 7 days end.

A second checkout has no trial. If you have already had a subscription, checkout does not add another 7 days free.

Cancel and refunds

Cancel in My Account. That stops the next renewal. Plus stays until the end of the current period. We do not give a partial refund of a period already charged, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires it.

Deleting the account cancels Plus straight away. That is different from cancel, which runs to the end of the period. What deletion removes, and what can remain, is on the data storage and deletion page.

Your information

The short version is on the homepage. The detail is on the privacy page. Hopewick does not claim Zero Data Retention, HIPAA, ISO, or encryption at rest. OpenAI receives the conversation content needed to reply. Training and data sharing for model improvement are off.
