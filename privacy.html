Privacy

This page says what Hopewick actually does with your information. It is not a promise of clinical confidentiality.

Who runs Hopewick

Hopewick is operated by BRIDGE&BITE.CO PTY LTD. Questions about privacy go to admin@bridge-bite-co.com.

What Hope is

Hope is an AI recovery companion. Hope is not a doctor, psychologist, registered counsellor, clinician, or emergency service. Chats are not monitored. Hopewick does not replace professional treatment or emergency care.

Staff do not get a feed of chats, and chats are not shared with a counsellor, an employer, or a service. That does not create the kind of confidentiality you have with a registered counsellor. The account data sits on the server that runs Hopewick.

On this device

The browser keeps a copy of chats, memories, check-ins, settings, and, where you use them, the journal, plans, and resume draft. A profile PIN is a light lock for a shared device, not strong security. Someone with access to that browser’s storage could still read what is saved there. You can export or clear your own data in Settings. Clearing the browser is separate from deleting the account.

An account

Sign in with email and password, or with Google. Apple sign-in is not offered. Magic link is the email login. On the sign-in screen it says “Email me a sign-in link”. You stay signed in on this browser until you sign out or you have not used Hopewick for 30 days.

When you set a password, Hopewick stores a scrypt password hash, not the password itself. The account can also hold your email, your name and phone if you add them, whether Plus is active, today’s message count, saved chats for each profile, check-ins, and weekly goals. Hopewick does not store your card number. Stripe collects the card.

Hope replies

You do not need your own API key. Free is 15 Hope messages a day. Hopewick Plus has no daily message cap. To write a reply, the conversation content needed for that reply is sent to OpenAI.

Training and data sharing for model improvement are off. That setting is not the same as Zero Data Retention. Zero Data Retention is not available. This page does not claim HIPAA, ISO, or encryption at rest.
