Hopewick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who we are

  • Hopewick is an app that helps people in recovery. It has a chat helper called Hope.
  • Hopewick is run by BRIDGE&BITE.CO PTY LTD (ABN 83 692 080 792).
  • In this policy, "we" and "us" means BRIDGE&BITE.CO PTY LTD.
  • We follow the Australian Privacy Act.
  • Some of what you tell us is about your health. The law calls this "sensitive information". We take extra care with it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email. Your name and phone, if you add them.
  • Your password: we keep a scrambled version only. We can't read it.
  • Your chats with Hope.
  • Your check-ins and goals.
  • Payment details: your Plus plan status and Stripe account number. We never see or keep your card number. Stripe does that.
  • Google sign-in: if you use it, Google tells us your email.
  • Use count: how many messages you sent today, and a total count of site visits. The visit count has no names in it.
  • On your device: the app also saves chats, notes, journal and settings in your own browser. We can't see that copy.

Why we collect it

  • To let you sign in and keep your account safe.
  • To show you your past chats and check-ins.
  • To let Hope reply to you.
  • To take payment for Plus.
  • To answer your emails and complaints.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use it for ads.
  • No person at Hopewick reads your chats as a normal part of running the app.

Who we share it with

We only share what is needed. We share with:

  • OpenAI (USA). Your chat is sent to OpenAI so Hope can write a reply. OpenAI does not use it to train its AI, based on our settings. OpenAI may keep it for a short time for safety checks.
  • Stripe (payments). Stripe gets your email and payment details if you buy Plus.
  • Google (sign-in). Only if you choose "Sign in with Google". Google does not see your chats.
  • Resend (email). Sends your sign-in link. It gets your email only.
  • Render (hosting). Our computer server is run by Render. Your account and chats are stored on a server in Singapore.
  • The law. We may share information if the law says we must, or to stop a serious threat to someone's life.

Overseas: OpenAI, Stripe, Google, Resend and Render may handle your information in other countries, mainly the USA. We pick companies that have strong privacy rules.

Young people

  • You must be 13 or older to use Hopewick.
  • If you are 13, 14 or 15, you need a parent or guardian's okay first.
  • We don't check ages. We trust you to tell the truth.
  • If we learn a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it.
  • A parent or guardian can email hello@hopewick.com.au to see or delete their child's info. We will check they are the parent first.

How we store and protect it

  • Your account and chats are kept on a private server in Singapore.
  • Only we can get into it.
  • Your connection to Hopewick is locked (HTTPS).
  • Passwords and sign-in links are scrambled.
  • We keep backup copies of the server.
  • No system is 100% safe. If there is a serious data breach, we will tell you and the OAIC.

How long we keep it

  • Chats: until you delete them, delete your account, or 2 years after the chat was last used.
  • Free plan: you only see your latest chat. Your older chats are still kept until the rules above apply.
  • Check-ins and goals: until you delete your account.
  • Backups: deleted info may stay in a backup for a short time, then it is gone.
  • Payment records: Stripe keeps invoices as the tax law requires.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your info

  • You can change your name and phone in Settings.
  • You can delete a chat in the app.
  • You can delete your whole account in Settings. This also stops your Plus plan.
  • Deleting your account does not clear the copy in your browser. Clear it in Settings on each device.
  • Want a copy of your info, or want us to fix or delete something? Email hello@hopewick.com.au.
  • We will reply within 30 days. It is free.

Complaints

  1. Email us first at hello@hopewick.com.au. Tell us what went wrong.
  2. We will reply within 30 days.
  3. Not happy with our answer? Contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC):
  4. Website: oaic.gov.au
  5. Phone: 1300 363 992

Contact
