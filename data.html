Your data

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This page is a short guide to where your information is kept, how long, and how to delete it. The full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

Where it is kept

  • Your account, chats, check-ins and goals are kept on a private server in Singapore.
  • The app also saves a copy in your own browser. We can't see that copy.
  • We never see or keep your card number. Stripe does that.

How long we keep it

  • Chats: until you delete them, delete your account, or 2 years after the chat was last used.
  • Free plan: you only see your latest chat. Your older chats are still kept until the rules above apply.
  • Check-ins and goals: until you delete your account.
  • Backups: deleted info may stay in a backup for a short time, then it is gone.
  • Payment records: Stripe keeps invoices as the tax law requires.

Deleting your info

  • You can delete a chat in the app.
  • You can delete your whole account in Settings. This also stops your Plus plan.
  • Deleting your account does not clear the copy in your browser. Clear it in Settings on each device.
  • Can't do it in the app? Want a copy of your info, or want us to fix or delete something? Email hello@hopewick.com.au.
  • We will reply within 30 days. It is free.

Parents and guardians

  • A parent or guardian can email hello@hopewick.com.au to see or delete their child's info. We will check they are the parent first.
