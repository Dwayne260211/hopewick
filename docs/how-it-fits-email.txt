Subject: How Hopewick fits in your AOD service

Hi,

Quick picture of how Hopewick works in a service like yours:

Your service stays in charge of care; Hopewick fills the quiet hours when people are on their own.

Hope is a companion for people in recovery between sessions — not a clinical decision-making tool and not a crisis line. Workers can still recommend it; Hope does not make treatment decisions.

Who does what
• Clients use Hope on their phone with an invite code
• Staff choose who gets a code, give a short brief, and decide whether to continue
• Bridge&Bite holds the AI key, issues codes, and applies usage caps
• Private by design — staff do not receive a feed of client conversations

A light pilot
1. Agree 4–6 weeks and 5–10 client invite codes
2. We email codes to your named contact
3. Staff brief: “between sessions; not counselling; not for emergencies”
4. Clients use the app on their own
5. Mid-pilot check-in, then continue the service, extend the pilot, or move to an ongoing licence

Staff time: ~15–30 minutes once to brief the team, 2–5 minutes per client code, no daily worker login.

We can share privacy-preserving pilot summaries (codes activated, general feature use, retention, anonymous feedback) — never private chat content.

Hopewick does not replace clinical judgement or your escalation pathways. Emergency: 000. National AOD Hotline: 1800 250 015.
