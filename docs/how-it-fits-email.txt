Hi,

Quick picture of how Hopewick works in a service like yours:

Hope (in Hopewick) is for people in recovery between sessions — not a clinician dashboard and not a crisis line. Your team stays in charge of care.

Who does what
• Clients use Hope on their phone with an invite code
• Staff choose who gets a code, give a short brief, and decide whether to keep the pilot
• Bridge&Bite holds the AI key, issues codes, and applies usage caps

A light pilot
1. Agree 4–6 weeks and 5–10 client invite codes
2. We email codes to your named contact
3. Staff brief: “between sessions; not counselling; not for emergencies”
4. Clients use the app on their own
5. One mid-pilot check-in, then continue / pause / paid seats

Staff time is small: ~15–30 minutes once to brief the team, a few minutes per client code, and no daily worker login.

Hopewick does not replace clinical judgement or your escalation pathways. Emergency: 000. National AOD Hotline: 1800 250 015.
