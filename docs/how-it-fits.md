## In one line
Hope is for people in recovery **between sessions**. Staff issue invite codes and brief clients — there is no clinician dashboard required for a small pilot.

## Who does what
- **Client:** uses Hope on their phone (invite code).
- **Staff:** choose who gets a code, give a short safety brief, decide whether to continue.
- **Bridge&Bite:** holds the AI key, issues codes, usage caps, email support.

## Simple pilot week
1. Agree 4–6 weeks / 5–10 client codes / one named contact.
2. Bridge&Bite emails codes to your contact.
3. Staff brief: between sessions; not counselling; not for emergencies.
4. Clients use the PWA on their own.
5. One mid-pilot check-in; then continue, pause, or paid seats.

## Sector fit
Complements counselling / case work / peer / residential step-down. Does **not** replace clinical judgement, crisis response, or duty. Does **not** give staff a live surveillance feed of client chats.

## Staff time
~15–30 min team brief once; 2–5 min per client code; one short mid-pilot check. No daily staff login.
