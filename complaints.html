Complaints

Write to admin@bridge-bite-co.com. Nothing in the app emails staff when you complain, and nothing in the app shows a chat to staff.

What we aim to do

We aim to acknowledge a written complaint within 2 business days and to resolve it within 10 business days. Those are targets, not a guarantee.

A reply will say what we will do, or what we cannot do. We will not promise a clinician review, a person watching chats, or a refund the Australian Consumer Law does not require. A period already charged is not partly refunded except where that law requires it.

Account deletion by email

Use My Account → Delete account when you can. Use email only when you cannot.

Send the request from the account’s sign-in email to admin@bridge-bite-co.com, and ask for the account to be deleted. A person handles it. It is not automatic. If the address does not match an account, we will say so and we will not delete a different account.

What that request is meant to remove: the sign-in, server chats, server check-ins, and weekly goals, and a current Plus subscription is cancelled straight away. You are signed out.

What can remain: a copy in the browser until you clear it in Settings; Stripe may keep invoices; existing Render disk snapshots can still hold older data. The request does not wipe a disk snapshot, and this does not promise they disappear at once.

On 3 October 2026 the newest snapshot was restored onto the live disk. Files were not compared one by one.

The storage rules, including the 24 month (730 day) chat limit, are on the data storage and deletion page.

This is not a crisis line

Hopewick is not an emergency service, and a complaint email is not monitored as a crisis line. If you or someone else is in danger, call 000. Other contacts are on the homepage safety list: Lifeline 13 11 14, Suicide Call Back Service 1300 659 467, National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline 1800 250 015, Adis QLD 1800 177 833, 1800RESPECT 1800 737 732, and 13YARN 13 92 76.
