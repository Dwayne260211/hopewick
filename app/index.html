        Before we start 🌿

        offers — an AI recovery companion for talking through alcohol and other drug use, cravings, and hard days. Faith content is optional. It draws on approaches used in Australian AOD services, but:

        • It is not a registered counsellor, doctor or clinician, and it can make mistakes.
        • It can't diagnose or advise on withdrawal, detox or medications. Stopping alcohol or benzodiazepines suddenly can be dangerous — please talk to a doctor.
        • In an emergency call 000. The Get help now button is always at the top of the screen.
        • Chats are stored only on this device, but messages are sent to your chosen AI provider to create replies.
