A recovery app built in Australia, for Australians in recovery

Looking for a drug and alcohol recovery app that understands Australian services, Australian slang and Australian crisis lines? Hopewick is a recovery companion built in Queensland by someone in recovery himself.

Hopewick is not a medical or crisis service. If you’re in danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support, call Lifeline on 13 11 14. In Queensland, Adis 24/7 Alcohol and Drug Support is on 1800 177 833.

What Hopewick is

Hopewick is a web app for people in drug and alcohol recovery, people working on their mental wellbeing, and anyone trying to rebuild their life one small step at a time. At its heart is Hope, an AI companion you can talk to when no one else is around. The idea is simple: keep hope lit between sessions, the way a wick keeps a flame alive.

It isn’t a rehab, a doctor or a counsellor. It’s something to sit alongside them, so you’re not on your own in the hours in between.

What you can do in the app

  • Talk to Hope about a craving, a slip, a hard hour or a tricky conversation you need to have.
  • Daily check-ins and a clean-time tracker with milestones that don’t wipe out your progress if you slip.
  • Journal and gratitudes with daily reflection prompts. See our gratitude journal for recovery guide.
  • Your safety plan, relapse prevention and exit plans, always free.
  • Coping tools like box breathing and circuit breakers for when things get loud.
  • Find meetings, treatment and AOD services, including NA, AA, SMART Recovery and state-based services.
  • Faith content if you want it, and hidden if you don’t.

Built for Australia, and for Queensland

Lots of recovery apps are made overseas and point you to services that don’t exist here. Hopewick uses Australian crisis numbers and services. In Queensland, that means Adis 24/7 Alcohol and Drug Support on 1800 177 833. Across Australia, the National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline on 1800 250 015 connects you to your state’s service, and Lifeline is on 13 11 14.

Hopewick is built on harm minimisation. It meets you where you’re at, whether you’re fresh out of detox, months into recovery, waiting on a rehab bed, or just starting to wonder whether your use is a problem.

Why I built it

I’m Dwayne Stevens. I grew up in foster care, was in jail by 17, and spent years with trauma and addiction running my life. In rehab, there were times when no caseworker was around and I had nowhere to put what I was feeling. Having AI to talk things through gave me the courage to go to staff and say I was struggling.

I’m now studying the Dual Diploma of Mental Health and Alcohol and Other Drugs through TAFE Queensland, with my graduation expected in January 2027. I’m not a doctor, psychologist or counsellor. I built Hopewick from lived experience, and you can read the full story on our story page.

Free to start

Hopewick Plus is AU$20 a month, with a 7-day free trial on your first subscription, and adds no daily limit on Hope messages (fair use applies), goals, full history and more. Your privacy

Your chats are yours. To write a reply, what you say is sent to an AI provider, and training on your data is switched off. You can see, edit or delete what Hope remembers. Read the plain-language privacy policy and how your data is stored.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a drug and alcohol recovery app made in Australia?

Yes. Hopewick is a recovery companion app built in Queensland by BRIDGE&BITE.CO PTY LTD. It uses Australian crisis lines and services, and points you to meetings and AOD services near you.

Is Hopewick an AOD support app for Queensland?

Hopewick works anywhere in Australia, and it includes Queensland services like Adis 24/7 Alcohol and Drug Support on 1800 177 833. It’s a support and reflection tool, not an AOD treatment service.

Do I need to download Hopewick?

No. Hopewick works in your phone or computer’s web browser at hopewick.com.au, and you can add it to your home screen.

Is Hopewick a replacement for rehab or counselling?

No. Hopewick is not a medical, clinical or crisis service and doesn’t provide treatment. It’s designed to sit alongside your doctor, counsellor, caseworker or rehab.

How much does Hopewick cost?

Hopewick Free is AU$0 with no card needed. Hopewick Plus is AU$20 a month, with a 7-day free trial on your first subscription, subject to eligibility. You can cancel anytime.

What should I do in a crisis?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support, call Lifeline on 13 11 14. In Queensland, Adis is available 24/7 on 1800 177 833.
