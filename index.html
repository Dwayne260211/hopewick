Why Hopewick exists

Built from lived experience. Created with purpose.

For years, trauma and addiction ran my life. I was the quietest person in the room. I thought I had to carry everything on my own.

That’s when I started using AI. It gave me somewhere to get my thoughts out, ask questions and work out what I was feeling when there was nobody around to talk to. It never replaced counselling, treatment or people. What it did was give me the courage to walk up to the staff on site and say, “I’m really struggling.”

My faith in Jesus Christ has played a significant role in my recovery. It gave me hope, purpose and a reason to keep going. But Hopewick isn’t about asking people to believe what I believe. Whatever you believe, you’re welcome here.

Dwayne Stevens, founder. In recovery myself. Completed the coursework and practical placement for the TAFE Queensland Dual Diploma of Mental Health and Alcohol and Other Drugs, graduation expected January 2027. I’m not a doctor, psychologist or registered counsellor.
