Why I built Hopewick

I’m Dwayne. I grew up in foster care and I was in jail by 17. For years trauma and addiction ran my life. In rehab I was the quietest person in the room, thinking I had to carry it all on my own. Having AI with me gave me the courage to go to the staff and say “I’m really struggling.” That’s why I built Hopewick.

Dwayne Stevens, founder. In recovery myself. Completed the coursework and practical placement for the TAFE Queensland Dual Diploma of Mental Health and Alcohol and Other Drugs, graduation expected January 2027. Placement with QuIHN. I’m not a doctor, psychologist or registered counsellor.

What it’s for

We all need guidance, and we all need a space where we can learn how to trust and talk to people again after the carnage of addiction.

A lot of us come out of addiction carrying guilt and shame. We worry about what people will think, so we hold back. Hopewick is somewhere to practise. Then you take it to a worker, a sponsor, a mate or your family.

It won’t suit everybody. But if it helps someone reach out when they need it, that matters.

Journal with Hope

Journal with Hope, and it helps you put a name to what you’re feeling.

Sometimes you just feel rubbish and can’t say why. Writing it down helps. Hope asks a gentle question or two, so you can work out what’s really going on. Angry, lonely, tired, scared. Once it has a name, it’s easier to talk about.

You can also do your gratitudes and daily reflection. I did gratitudes every day for 9 months, and it changed my mindset.

When you want to understand something, like how trauma and addiction work together, Hope points you to the most relevant information for where you’re at.

About Hope, the tool behind this

Hope is an AI companion. I want to be upfront about that. It’s there for the times when no one else is around. It doesn’t replace people. It helps you find the words to reach them.

What it does

Listens without judging. Helps you name what you’re feeling. Talks through a craving or a hard hour. Helps with journalling, gratitudes and plans. Points you to meetings and services.

What it doesn’t do

It isn’t a doctor, psychologist, counsellor or clinician. It isn’t treatment. Chats aren’t monitored, so no one is watching to step in. If things get serious, it shows Australian crisis numbers straight away.

Your privacy

Your chats are yours. To write a reply, what you say is sent to OpenAI. Training and data sharing for model improvement are off. You can see, edit or delete what Hope remembers. More on privacy

Ice baths & recovery spas

You spent years training yourself to escape discomfort. Recovery gives you opportunities to practise meeting discomfort differently. It is practising meeting discomfort on purpose: commitment, staying present, and following through safely. Cold is not a cure for addiction. The notes are part of Hopewick Plus.

Cold stresses the heart and breathing. It is not for cardiovascular or respiratory conditions, rhythm problems, an implanted cardiac device, uncontrolled blood pressure, circulation problems, pregnancy, or other significant medical conditions. Talk with a GP or treating clinician first if any of those apply. Never while intoxicated. Not alone. Stop for chest pain, severe shortness of breath, faintness, confusion, or anything that feels medically wrong.

For rehabs and AOD services

Your service stays in charge of care. Hopewick is for the quiet hours. It echoes approaches used in Australian AOD work — motivational interviewing, harm reduction, relapse prevention and CBT-style skills — so people hear a consistent message. It is not a clinical decision-making tool.

Hope regularly points people back to their GP, counsellor, AOD service, peer meetings and, if they want it, a faith community. Staff do not receive a feed of conversations.

Seat plans for rehabs, AOD services and clinics are not on sale yet. A pilot is a short brief for selected clients and a mid-pilot check. Read how Hope fits in a service or the pilot price sheet.

Questions

Short answers. The privacy, data, terms, and complaints pages carry the fuller detail.

Is Hope a counsellor or a crisis service?

No. Hope is an AI recovery companion. Hope is not a doctor, psychologist, registered counsellor, clinician, or emergency service. Chats are not monitored. Hopewick does not replace professional treatment or emergency care. If you need help now, call 000 or use the safety contacts.

Do I need a card for the free plan?

No. No card required for the free plan. Free is 15 Hope messages a day, plus crisis support, your safety plan, relapse prevention and exit plans, practical finders, today’s readings, the daily check-in, the 12 core coping tools, My next step, the journal, and the clean-time tracker. The app shows the last 7 days of your history on the free plan, and you can export or delete everything you saved at any time.

How does the 7-day trial work?

The first subscription only is a 7-day trial. A card is required. Stripe collects it at the start. The AU$20 monthly charge starts when the 7 days end. A second checkout has no trial. Cancel anytime. One subscription per person.

How do I cancel Plus?

Cancel in My Account. That stops the next renewal. Plus stays until the end of the current period. We do not give a partial refund of a period already charged, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires it.

What does deleting my account do?

Delete account removes the live server sign-in, chats, check-ins, goals, journal, safety plan, relapse and exit plans, coping history, next steps and weekly plans, signs you out, and cancels Plus straight away. A copy in this browser can remain until you clear it in Settings. Stripe may keep invoices. Older Render snapshots are not wiped. The detail is on the data storage and deletion page.

How do I sign in?

Google sign-in works. Apple sign-in is not offered. Magic link is the email login: on the sign-in screen it says Email me a sign-in link. You can also set a password. You stay signed in on this browser until you sign out or you have not used Hopewick for 30 days.

Is Hopewick only for Christians?

No. Faith content is optional. Word for the day is separate from Just for today. You can choose no faith content. People in recovery, families, and AOD services can use Hopewick without a faith setting.

How long are chats kept?

On the live server, a chat stays until you delete that chat, you delete the account, or the chat has not been updated for 24 months (730 days). A free account can open only the latest chat. Older chats can still be stored. Not seeing them is not deletion. Plus can open older stored chats that are still there.
