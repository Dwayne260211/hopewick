Who it's for

Designed to complement treatment, not replace it. It's there in the hours between appointments, when cravings and hard moments often happen.

🏡

Rehabs & AOD services

Offer clients a consistent, values-aligned support tool they can use between sessions, after discharge, or while waiting for a place.

🧑‍⚕️

Counsellors & clinicians

Reinforce the skills you teach, like urge surfing, HALT checks, coping plans and SMART goals, with a companion that uses the same language.

💚

Clients & families

A private, judgement-free space to talk through cravings, slips, goals, faith and hope — plus practical nearby help when food or shelter is the need.

Key features

How it supports clinicians

Between-session support that echoes evidence-based practice used in Australian AOD services, so clients hear consistent messages.

  • Motivational interviewing: open questions, affirmations, reflections and summaries. It works with ambivalence and follows the stages of change.
  • Harm reduction: meets people where they are. Abstinence, cutting down and safer use all count.
  • Relapse prevention: triggers and high-risk situations, HALT, urge surfing, coping plans and support networks.
  • CBT-style skills: gently noticing and reframing unhelpful thoughts, and planning alternatives.
  • Strengths-based, trauma-informed, culturally safe: person-first, non-stigmatising language, with choice and control.
  • Workforce learning links: Info & training points staff to Insight, Dovetail, NCETA and other official AU AOD training hubs (link-outs only).

Designed to point back to you

Hopewick regularly encourages people to stay connected with their GP, counsellor, AOD service, faith community and peer groups such as SMART Recovery, AA/NA or Celebrate Recovery. It's a bridge between sessions, not a substitute for them.

Service-level features such as clinician-configured goals and plans, and optional summaries shared with the client's consent, are ideas we'd like to shape with pilot partners.

Safety first

If someone mentions suicide, self-harm, overdose, being unsafe or someone else in danger, a crisis card with Australian contacts appears straight away. This works even without an internet connection to the AI. The companion is also instructed to give these contacts first.

Not a replacement for clinical care. It's an AI support tool, not a registered counsellor, doctor or crisis service. It doesn't diagnose, and it doesn't give medical advice on withdrawal, detox or medications. It explains that stopping alcohol or benzodiazepines suddenly can be dangerous and needs a doctor.

Privacy: how it works today

We'd rather be plain about this than overpromise.

Stays on the device

Chats, memories, check-ins and settings are stored only in the browser on the device being used. There are no accounts and no server of ours holding conversations.

Demo sends nothing

The public demo uses pre-written examples only — nothing you type is sent to an AI provider. When hosted accounts open, replies will use our managed service instead of asking visitors for keys.

Service-ready hosting ROADMAP

A hosted version for services, with Australian data storage and managed access, is on the roadmap. It isn't available yet.

Profile PINs are a light privacy lock for shared devices, not strong security. Anyone with access to the browser's storage could still read the data. People can export or delete their own data at any time.

Advanced (in the app): optional own OpenAI-compatible API key for device testing — tucked under Settings → Advanced, not the main path.

Faith-inclusive by design

For many people, faith is a real source of strength in recovery. For others it isn't. Each person chooses what feels right:

General spiritualityChristianCatholicIslamicJewishBuddhistHinduSikhIndigenous spiritualityNo faith content
  • Faith can come up only when asked, be gently woven in, or be central.
  • Never preachy or shaming, and never treats addiction or relapse as a moral failing.
  • Paraphrases sacred texts, and says so, rather than risk misquoting them.
  • For Indigenous spirituality, it respects culture without appropriating it and defers to Elders, community and 13YARN.
