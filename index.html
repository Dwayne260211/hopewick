Who it's for

People in recovery can use Hope on their own. Rehabs and AOD services can offer it between sessions. It complements treatment. It does not replace a counsellor, a doctor, or a crisis service. In an emergency call 000.

People in recovery

A private, judgement-free place to talk through cravings, slips, goals, faith and hope. You can start on your own phone — you don’t need a service to try Hope.

Somewhere calm to think things through, plus practical nearby help when food or shelter is the need.

Between sessions, aligned with care

Your service stays in charge of care; Hopewick fills the quiet hours when people are on their own. Hope echoes approaches used in Australian AOD work so clients hear consistent messages — it is not a clinical decision-making tool.

  • Motivational interviewing: open questions, affirmations, reflections and summaries. It works with ambivalence and follows the stages of change.
  • Harm reduction: meets people where they are. Abstinence, cutting down and safer use all count.
  • Relapse prevention: triggers and high-risk situations, HALT, urge surfing, coping plans and support networks.
  • CBT-style skills: gently noticing and reframing unhelpful thoughts, and planning alternatives.
  • Strengths-based, trauma-informed, culturally safe: person-first, non-stigmatising language, with choice and control.

Designed to point back to your service

Hopewick regularly encourages people to stay connected with their GP, counsellor, AOD service, faith community and peer groups such as SMART Recovery, AA/NA or Celebrate Recovery. It's a bridge between sessions, not a substitute for them.

Private by design — staff do not receive a feed of client conversations. A small pilot is a short brief for selected clients and a mid-pilot check — see how it fits in an AOD service.

Safety first

If someone mentions suicide or self-harm, overdose, about to use / extreme craving, domestic or family violence, being unsafe, or someone else in danger, a crisis card appears straight away with what to do in this moment and Australian call buttons. This works even without an internet connection to the AI. Hope is also instructed to give these contacts first.

Not a replacement for clinical care. Hope is an AI recovery companion, not a registered counsellor, doctor or crisis service. It doesn't diagnose, and it doesn't give medical advice on withdrawal, detox or medications. It explains that stopping alcohol or benzodiazepines suddenly can be dangerous and needs a doctor.

Stays on the device

Chats, memories, check-ins and settings stay in the browser on the device being used. If you sign in for Hopewick Plus, we store your email and subscription status — not your conversations.

Your account

Hopewick Plus sign-in is an email link. We keep the email and whether Plus is active. Conversations are not uploaded.

Faith-inclusive by design

For many people, faith is a real source of strength in recovery. For others it isn't. Each person chooses what feels right:

General spiritualityChristianCatholicIslamicJewishBuddhistHinduSikhIndigenous spiritualityNo faith content
  • Faith can come up only when asked, be gently woven in, or be central.
  • Never preachy or shaming, and never treats addiction or relapse as a moral failing.
  • Paraphrases sacred texts, and says so, rather than risk misquoting them.
  • For Indigenous spirituality, it respects culture without appropriating it and defers to Elders, community and 13YARN.

COMING SOON

A little more company for the quiet hours between appointments. Practices drawn from CBT (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy) and DBT (Dialectical Behaviour Therapy) are on their way into Hopewick — skills you can try beside the people who already walk with you.

💭

CBT

A calm way to notice a thought that makes a craving or a hard moment feel like the whole story, check it gently, and choose a smaller next step. At your pace, with no grades.

🌊

DBT

For the minutes when a feeling or an urge is loud: a pause, a breath, and a small action you can choose. Something steady to hold, then you can come back to your day.

When they arrive, these will be self-help style tools in the app — practice you can open, alongside a counsellor, psychologist, sponsor, or GP.
