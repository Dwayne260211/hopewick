Remembers what matters

Goals, triggers, supports and what helps can be kept so conversations build over time. You can view, edit or delete every memory.

Nutrition & rebuilding

Explore food, hydration, supplements and different approaches people consider while rebuilding their physical wellbeing. Understand what evidence says, what remains uncertain and when professional guidance matters.

Hopewick separates established evidence, emerging or limited evidence, individual experience, and possible risks or interactions. Limited evidence is not a reason to dismiss a question. It is a reason to stay honest. No supplement, diet or lifestyle practice here is a cure for addiction. Strong warnings stay where they matter: high-risk substances, medicine interactions, serious medical conditions, and dangerous withdrawal from alcohol or benzodiazepines.

Gut health & recovery

The gut–brain connection, fermented foods, fibre, sleep and the habits that change them while you rebuild. Education only. It does not treat addiction. A GP or dietitian matters if you have a gut condition, take regular medicine, or something feels seriously wrong — not after every ordinary meal.

Building new brain habits

How habits, thoughts and recovery actually work: flexibility, learning, cravings and connection. A thought is not an instruction. Not a treatment, and not a claim that mindset is enough.

Ice baths & recovery spas

You spent years training yourself to escape discomfort. Recovery gives you opportunities to practise meeting discomfort differently. Cold exposure is not a claim that cold cures addiction. It is practising meeting discomfort on purpose: commitment, staying present, and following through safely.

The win is not how long you stay in the water. The win is making a commitment to yourself, facing the first urge to get out, staying present and following through safely. Some people feel more alert afterwards. Experiences vary.

Cold stresses the heart and breathing. It is not for cardiovascular or respiratory conditions, rhythm problems, an implanted cardiac device, uncontrolled blood pressure, circulation problems, pregnancy, or other significant medical conditions. Talk with a GP or treating clinician first if any of those apply. Never while intoxicated. Not alone. Stop for chest pain, severe shortness of breath, faintness, confusion, or anything that feels medically wrong.

Private by design

Your conversations are not shared with a counsellor, employer or service. When you use an account, your saved chats are stored with that account, and kept in this browser, so they are there when you return.

Read how your information is handled

On this device

Chats, memories, check-ins and settings stay in the browser you are using. A profile PIN is a light lock for a shared device, not strong security. Someone with access to that browser’s storage could still read what is saved there. You can export or delete your own data.

With an account

Sign in with your email and password, and stay signed in on this browser until you sign out or you have not used Hopewick for 30 days. Email me a sign-in link is there if you are new or you have forgotten the password. We keep the email, a password hash when you set a password (not the password itself), whether Plus is active, saved chats for each profile, and signed-in check-ins and weekly goals. A chat is kept until you delete that chat, delete the account, or it has not been updated for 24 months, and then it is removed from the live server. A free account can open the latest chat. Older chats can still be stored. Not seeing them is not deletion. Hopewick Plus can open stored history that is still there. Deleting the account removes the sign-in, server chats, check-ins, and weekly goals, and signs you out. A copy in this browser can remain until you clear it in Settings. Stripe can keep invoices. We do not store your card number. Hopewick staff do not get a feed of those chats.

Hope replies

Sign in and Hope replies on Hopewick. Free is 15 messages a day. Hopewick Plus has no daily message limit. You do not need your own API key.

Complaints, privacy questions, and a request to delete an account you cannot delete in the app: email admin@bridge-bite-co.com. We aim to acknowledge a written complaint within 2 business days and to resolve it within 10 business days. Those are targets, not a guarantee. Deleting an account in the app removes the sign-in, server chats, check-ins, and weekly goals, and signs you out. It does not necessarily remove every copy at once. A copy in this browser can remain until you clear it in Settings. Stripe can keep invoices. Existing Render disk snapshots can still hold older data. Deleting an account does not wipe them, and this does not promise they disappear at once. On 3 October 2026 the newest snapshot was restored onto the live disk. Files were not compared one by one.

Safety built in

When serious safety concerns appear, Hopewick immediately surfaces verified Australian crisis and emergency contacts. Hopewick provides support between sessions. It is not a doctor, counsellor or emergency service.

Faith, if you want it

Choose Christian, another faith tradition, general spirituality or no faith content. You control how much faith appears. It is never used to shame, and a lapse is not treated as a moral failing. Study options stay in the app.

For rehabs and AOD services

Your service stays in charge of care. Hopewick is for the quiet hours. It echoes approaches used in Australian AOD work — motivational interviewing, harm reduction, relapse prevention and CBT-style skills — so people hear a consistent message. It is not a clinical decision-making tool.

Hope regularly points people back to their GP, counsellor, AOD service, peer meetings and, if they want it, a faith community. Staff do not receive a feed of conversations.

Seat plans for rehabs, AOD services and clinics are not on sale yet. A pilot is a short brief for selected clients and a mid-pilot check. Read how Hope fits in a service or the pilot price sheet.

Why I built Hopewick

I’m Dwayne Stevens. My understanding of recovery comes from lived experience as well as formal study. I grew up in foster care where abuse, trauma and substance use were part of the environment around me. I spent much of my life caught in addiction, incarceration and periods where I did not want to keep living.

Recovery changed the direction of my life. Faith became central to that change, and it eventually led me into studying mental health and alcohol and other drugs because I wanted to use what I had lived through to help other people.

I have completed a Dual Diploma in Mental Health and Alcohol and Other Drugs. Hopewick was built from the belief that support should not disappear when an appointment ends. People still have cravings, questions, setbacks, decisions and difficult hours to get through.

Hopewick is not here to replace counsellors, doctors, peer workers or recovery communities. It is here to provide another layer of support between those moments.

Dwayne Stevens

Founder, Hopewick
