Nutrition & rebuilding

Nutrition notes — rebuild the basics, and explore food and supplements with clear evidence. These notes are part of Hopewick Plus.

Hopewick separates established evidence, emerging or limited evidence, individual experience, and possible risks or interactions. No supplement, diet or lifestyle practice here is a cure for addiction.

Gut health & recovery

The gut–brain connection, fermented foods, fibre, sleep and the habits that change them while you rebuild. Education only. It does not treat addiction. Gut health and neuroplasticity notes are part of Plus.

Building new brain habits

How habits, thoughts and recovery actually work. A thought is not an instruction. Not a treatment, and not a claim that mindset is enough. These notes are part of Plus.

Ice baths & recovery spas

You spent years training yourself to escape discomfort. Recovery gives you opportunities to practise meeting discomfort differently. It is practising meeting discomfort on purpose: commitment, staying present, and following through safely. Cold is not a cure for addiction. The notes are part of Hopewick Plus.

Cold stresses the heart and breathing. It is not for cardiovascular or respiratory conditions, rhythm problems, an implanted cardiac device, uncontrolled blood pressure, circulation problems, pregnancy, or other significant medical conditions. Talk with a GP or treating clinician first if any of those apply. Never while intoxicated. Not alone. Stop for chest pain, severe shortness of breath, faintness, confusion, or anything that feels medically wrong.

I’m Dwayne Stevens. My understanding of recovery comes from lived experience as well as formal study. I grew up in foster care where abuse, trauma and substance use were part of the environment around me. I spent much of my life caught in addiction, incarceration and periods where I did not want to keep living.

Recovery changed the direction of my life. Faith became central to that change, and it eventually led me into studying mental health and alcohol and other drugs because I wanted to use what I had lived through to help other people.

I have completed a Dual Diploma in Mental Health and Alcohol and Other Drugs. Hopewick was built from the belief that support should not disappear when an appointment ends. People still have cravings, questions, setbacks, decisions and difficult hours to get through.

Hopewick is not here to replace counsellors, doctors, peer workers or recovery communities. It is here to provide another layer of support between those moments. Faith is part of my story. Hopewick is not only for Christians.

Your information, in short

Chats are not shared with a counsellor, an employer, or a service, and Hopewick staff do not get a feed of them. Chats are not monitored. That is not the same as a clinical duty of confidentiality, and this page does not promise one.

Sign in with email and password, or with Google. Email me a sign-in link is there if you are new or you have forgotten the password. You stay signed in on this browser until you sign out or you have not used Hopewick for 30 days. Apple sign-in is not offered.

With an account, saved chats, check-ins, and weekly goals are stored with that account and kept in this browser. To write a reply, the conversation content needed for that reply is sent to OpenAI. Training and data sharing for model improvement are off. Zero Data Retention is not available.

Faith, if you want it

Faith content is optional. Word for the day is a short Christian reading, and it is separate from Just for today. You can choose Christian, another tradition, general spirituality, or no faith content for how Hope talks with you. Hopewick is not only for Christians. Faith is never used to shame, and a lapse is not treated as a moral failing.

For rehabs and AOD services

Your service stays in charge of care. Hopewick is for the quiet hours. It echoes approaches used in Australian AOD work — motivational interviewing, harm reduction, relapse prevention and CBT-style skills — so people hear a consistent message. It is not a clinical decision-making tool.

Hope regularly points people back to their GP, counsellor, AOD service, peer meetings and, if they want it, a faith community. Staff do not receive a feed of conversations.

Seat plans for rehabs, AOD services and clinics are not on sale yet. A pilot is a short brief for selected clients and a mid-pilot check. Read how Hope fits in a service or the pilot price sheet.

Questions

Short answers. The privacy, data, terms, and complaints pages carry the fuller detail.

Is Hope a counsellor or a crisis service?

No. Hope is an AI recovery companion. Hope is not a doctor, psychologist, registered counsellor, clinician, or emergency service. Chats are not monitored. Hopewick does not replace professional treatment or emergency care. If you need help now, call 000 or use the safety contacts.

Do I need a card for the free plan?

No. No card required for the free plan. Free is 15 Hope messages a day, plus crisis support, practical finders, today’s readings, the daily check-in, and the clean-time tracker.

How does the 7-day trial work?

The first subscription only is a 7-day trial. A card is required. Stripe collects it at the start. The AU$20 monthly charge starts when the 7 days end. A second checkout has no trial. Cancel anytime. One subscription per person.

How do I cancel Plus?

Cancel in My Account. That stops the next renewal. Plus stays until the end of the current period. We do not give a partial refund of a period already charged, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires it.

What does deleting my account do?

Delete account removes the live server sign-in, chats, check-ins, and weekly goals, signs you out, and cancels Plus straight away. A copy in this browser can remain until you clear it in Settings. Stripe may keep invoices. Older Render snapshots are not wiped. The detail is on the data storage and deletion page.

How do I sign in?

Google sign-in works. Apple sign-in is not offered. Magic link is the email login: on the sign-in screen it says Email me a sign-in link. You can also set a password. You stay signed in on this browser until you sign out or you have not used Hopewick for 30 days.

Is Hopewick only for Christians?

No. Faith content is optional. Word for the day is separate from Just for today. You can choose no faith content. People in recovery, families, and AOD services can use Hopewick without a faith setting.

How long are chats kept?

On the live server, a chat stays until you delete that chat, you delete the account, or the chat has not been updated for 24 months (730 days). A free account can open only the latest chat. Older chats can still be stored. Not seeing them is not deletion. Plus can open older stored chats that are still there.
