How Hopewick fits in an AOD service

Hope is a companion for people in recovery between sessions — not a clinician tool and not a crisis line. Your service stays in charge of care; Hopewick fills the quiet hours when people are on their own.

Who does what

Client / participant

Opens Hopewick on their phone, redeems an invite code, and talks with Hope when they need encouragement, reflection, or a calm next step between appointments.

Staff / service

Decide who gets a code, brief them in one conversation, and decide whether to keep the pilot. You do not need a second staff dashboard to run a small pilot.

A simple week in your organisation

Pilot shape

Light lift for the service

  1. Agree the pilot — 4–6 weeks, 5–10 client invite codes, one named contact at your service.
  2. Bridge&Bite issues codes — emailed to your contact (company holds the AI key; no personal OpenAI keys).
  3. Staff brief clients — “This is Hope, between sessions. It’s not counselling and not for emergencies. If you’re in crisis, call 000 / your usual pathways.”
  4. Clients use Hope on their own phone — home-screen PWA; chat, reflection, and support-oriented resources in the app.
  5. You check in once — mid-pilot: any issues? anyone using it? then decide continue / pause / paid seats.

Where it sits in the sector

  • Complements counselling, case work, peer support, and residential programs — the gap between face-to-face contact.
  • Does not replace clinical judgement, risk assessment, medication decisions, or after-hours duty.
  • Does not monitor clients for you — no staff surveillance feed in the current product.
  • Fits outpatient, community, residential step-down, and youth AOD settings that already brief people on safe self-help tools.

What staff actually do (time)

  • Kick-off briefing: about 15–30 minutes once for the team.
  • Handing a code to a client: about 2–5 minutes.
  • Mid-pilot check with Bridge&Bite: one short email or call.
  • No daily login required for workers.

Safety: Hopewick is not medical advice and not a crisis service. In an emergency call 000. National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline: 1800 250 015. Your service’s existing escalation pathways still apply.
